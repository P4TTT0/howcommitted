**How committed is @you?** Type a GitHub username and watch its contribution history play back as sound and motion, year by year.

**[Try it at howcommitted.com](https://www.howcommitted.com/)**

<img width="960" alt="A contribution history playing back in Committed" src="docs/assets/readme.gif" />

## What you get

- **Your history, played.** Every contribution adds up on one timeline, and the sound climbs with it.
- **Scenes to explore.** Pause, skip or change speed. Every finished chart is interactive.
- **Versus.** Add up to 5 developers and play their histories side by side.
- **Share cards.** Save your run as images ready for stories.

## Contributions are not productivity

Committed shows rhythm and habits, never value. Versus is a game, not a job ranking.

## Privacy

Public data only. Private contributions appear only if you share them on your GitHub profile. Signing in with GitHub is optional, asks for no extra permissions and only shows up after the free runs are used.

Visits are counted with Vercel Web Analytics: no cookies, no cross-site tracking, only page views with referrer, country and device.
